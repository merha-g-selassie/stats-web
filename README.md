# Basics of statistics

A static website covering the basics of statistics. Its goal is to make essential concepts easy to understand through short explanations, concrete examples, step-by-step calculations, and interactive visualizations. The chapters and topics are intentionally selected around my own learning needs rather than aiming to provide a complete statistics curriculum, and new material will be added over time as I encounter new concepts I need to learn.
